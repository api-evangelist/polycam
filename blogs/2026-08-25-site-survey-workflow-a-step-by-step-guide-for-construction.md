A typical site survey workflow begins with planning and finishes with a file ready for BIM or GIS. The tools used for this process have changed significantly in recent years. This guide walks you through each step: planning, on-site capture, real-time quality checks, cleanup, export, and moving a mobile scan into Revit, Civil 3D, or CloudCompare.
